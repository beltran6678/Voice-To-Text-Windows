## ❓ The Problem

Typing takes time. Speaking is faster. Writing emails, documents, or notes manually is slow. You want to speak and see text appear instantly.

This tool turns your voice into text. Speak into your mic – text appears anywhere.

---

## ⚡ Features

- **Real-time transcription** – speak and see text appear instantly
- **Offline** – no internet required, your voice stays local
- **Auto-paste** – text automatically pastes into any active window
- **Multilingual** – supports 20+ languages
- **No subscriptions** – completely free

---

## 📥 Download & Installation

1. Download the latest version from **[Releases](../../releases)**
2. Unzip with password: **`68924`**
