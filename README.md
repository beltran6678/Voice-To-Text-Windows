1. **Open the app:** Double-click the downloaded file. A small window will appear.
2. **Connect a microphone:** Plug in or enable your microphone (built-in or external). Windows should detect it automatically.
3. **Start dictation:** Click the microphone button or press a hotkey (like Ctrl+Shift+D) to begin listening. Speak clearly. The app will type your words into the active text field.
